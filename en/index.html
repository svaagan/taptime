TapTime

Say a word. Tap the letter. Fast!

TapTime is a party game for 2–8 players around one iPhone. The phone sits in the middle of the table, and you get a category. Take turns saying a word that fits and starts with a free letter. Tap that letter to pass the turn and reset the clock. Run out of time and you’re out.

ABKST

How to play

  1. Put the phone in the middle of the tableEnter the players’ names. Everyone sits around it and taps the same screen.
  2. Draw a cardThe card gives you the category, like “Things in the ocean” or “Countries of the world.”
  3. Say a word and tap its first letterThe word has to fit the card and start with a free letter. Your tap locks the letter, passes the turn, and resets the clock.
  4. Last one standing winsRun out of time and you’re out of the round. The last player left gets the point.

Free to play

45 cards are free, and you can play the full game without buying anything. The app is in English and Norwegian. No account, no ads, and the app collects nothing about you.
